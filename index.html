How It Works

From Friction to a Clear Reply
in Three Controlled Steps.

01

Scan the Page

ORBIT reads relevant comment content on supported pages while you browse. It does not monitor unrelated websites.

02

Assess Risk

Sentiment, urgency and refund language are combined into a simple 0–100 risk score so critical messages surface first.

03

Review and Reply

ORBIT drafts a contextual answer. You remain in control: review, edit, then post the response yourself.
