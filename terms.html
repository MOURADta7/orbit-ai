Last updated: May 7, 2026

1. About ORBIT AI

ORBIT AI is a Chrome extension for SaaS founders and digital product creators. It helps users detect refund-risk comments on supported product platforms and generate professional AI-powered reply drafts for customer support and product communication.

2. Acceptable Use

ORBIT AI may only be used for legitimate customer support, business communication, review management, and product-related replies.

3. Prohibited Use

You may not use ORBIT AI to generate, request, publish, or distribute:

  • NSFW, sexually explicit, or sexually suggestive content.
  • Harassing, abusive, hateful, threatening, or discriminatory content.
  • Illegal, deceptive, fraudulent, or misleading content.
  • Spam, phishing, impersonation, or manipulation of reviews.
  • Content that violates the rights, privacy, or intellectual property of others.
  • Content intended to bypass platform rules, laws, or safety systems.

4. AI Output

AI-generated replies are drafts. Users are responsible for reviewing, editing, and approving any reply before posting it publicly.
